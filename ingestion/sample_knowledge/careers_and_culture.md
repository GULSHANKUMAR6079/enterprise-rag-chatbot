# Careers & Engineering Culture

## Engineering Values
At Incerro, we build for durability, performance, and uncompromising safety. We believe in:
- Deterministic Guardrails over probabilistic hopes
- Defense in Depth across every OSI layer
- Automated testing and adversarial red-teaming as core development rituals
- Continuous learning and transparent post-mortems
