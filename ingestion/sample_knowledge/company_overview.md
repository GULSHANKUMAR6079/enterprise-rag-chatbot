# Company Overview: Incerro Enterprise Technologies

## Mission and Vision
Incerro Enterprise Technologies provides next-generation enterprise infrastructure for secure Artificial Intelligence applications, digital engineering, and modern web architectures. Founded in 2021, our mission is to empower global organizations to deploy AI assistants, agentic workflows, and hybrid retrieval systems with deterministic security boundaries and zero data leakage.

## Corporate Entity and Group Affiliation
- **Legal Corporate Entity**: Incerro is operated by and is an official entity of **Invasion Software Technologies Pvt Ltd** (registered parent company).
- **Group Companies**: Incerro is affiliated with the **CodeBin Group** (CodeBin), sharing collaborative engineering hubs and technology labs.
- **Global Presence**: Headquartered in Pune, India, with international operations in Dubai (UAE) and the United States (USA).

## Core Capabilities & Services
Incerro specializes in:
- Multi-Layered AI Gateway Architecture and Secure Enterprise Chatbots
- Production-Grade Defense Against Prompt Injection and Jailbreaks
- High-Performance Hybrid Retrieval (Dense Vector Embeddings + BM25 Reciprocal Rank Fusion)
- Headless CMS Development & Migration (Official Sanity.io Agency Partner)
- Enterprise Full-Stack Engineering, Next.js, and Spatial XR Interfaces
